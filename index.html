About Me

As a child, I dreamed of someday owning Disney World. Life had other plans. Over time, my interests shifted toward medicine, engineering, and entrepreneurship, leading me to a career focused on developing medical devices and related healthcare technologies.

I studied biomedical engineering at the University at Buffalo before continuing at the University of Rochester's Center for Medical Technology and Innovation. Today, I serve as CEO of Portico and work with Rookery Labs as part of Egret Healthcare Ventures. Across these roles, I help identify clinical needs and turn promising ideas into medical devices, technologies, and businesses.

I believe meaningful innovation happens when engineers, clinicians, entrepreneurs, and patients work together. My goal is to bring those perspectives together to create practical solutions that improve patient care and outcomes.

I may never become the sole owner of the "Happiest Place on Earth," but I do own one share of The Walt Disney Company. Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, water and winter sports, photography, and 3D printing.
